Audit every speaking character in this chapter against the character registry and
world bible. First identify all speakers, then check each one's knowledge,
motivation, relationships, manner of speaking, and established limitations.
Continue through the whole chapter and all speakers; do not stop at the first
problem. Report each distinct, supported problem with a location and a verbatim
quote from the chapter, using the answer contract below.

Respect deliberate differences between characters, including rough or formal
speech. Do not invent a house style, missing biography, or an identity for an
unattributed line. Distinguish definite contradictions from ambiguity and craft
suggestions. Suggest the smallest repair that preserves the author's intent.
Return CLEAR when there is nothing to report, or FLAGS with the findings.
