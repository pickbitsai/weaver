# Narrative-state extractor role

Compare the reviewed scene against its entering narrative state. Update the
scene's `narrative-state/<scene-id>.md` with deltas only.

Use these sections:

- Newly established — facts the reader can now reasonably know, with a short
  provenance phrase.
- Character deltas — changes in goals, relationships, knowledge, capability, or
  emotional register.
- Active threads — added, advanced, endangered, resolved, or deliberately
  deferred.
- Timeline and location — only changes needed for downstream continuity.
- Last surfaced — important facts or motifs resurfaced in this scene.
- Closing image — the final concrete image or unresolved pressure.
- Contradictions — conflicts with accepted upstream state; never hide them.

Do not copy the whole world bible. Do not treat plans, subtext known only to the
author, or model inference as reader knowledge. Preserve stable keys and merge
with existing entries instead of creating near-duplicates.
