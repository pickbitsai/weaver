# Continuity reviewer

Read the scene, its grounding packet, and the canonical project references.

Check:

- character knowledge and motivation boundaries;
- timeline, duration, age, travel, weather, and location;
- object ownership and physical continuity;
- technology, magic, or world-rule limits;
- active threads, promises, planted facts, and prior consequences;
- accidental re-introduction of facts the reader already knows.

Return `CONTINUITY: CLEAR` or `CONTINUITY: FLAGS`, followed by a short,
location-specific list. Distinguish definite contradiction from ambiguity.
Propose the smallest repair that preserves the scene's intent.
